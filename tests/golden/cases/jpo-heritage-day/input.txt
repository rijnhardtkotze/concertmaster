PAGE TITLE: Heritage Day Symphonic Jazz - Johannesburg Philharmonic Orchestra

Heritage Day Symphonic Jazz
DATE: Thursday, 24 September 2026
TIME: 19h00
VENUE: The Linder Auditorium
PROGRAMME:
Works by Duke Ellington, Leonard Bernstein, George Gershwin, Victor Masondo and Lindi Ngonelo
Conductor: Daniel Boico
Soloists: Victor Masondo and Lindi Ngonelo
Join us as we present the annual JPO Symphonic Jazz concert 2026 taking place on 24 September at 7.00pm at the Linder Auditorium.
Conducted by Daniel Boico, the programme will feature Duke Elington’s Nutcracker Suite and will feature South African jazz veterans Lindi Ngonelo and Victor Masondo’s Quartet.
